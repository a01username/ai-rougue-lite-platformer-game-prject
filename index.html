Movement field notes +

Downward movementPress Down Arrow in the air to attack down and pogo on enemies or projectiles. Hold S + Space to drop through a thin platform. Crouching keeps you from walking off an edge.

Walls & ledgesTouch visible rock to slide; open cliff edges cannot be gripped. Hold toward rock to slide slower, or hold S to stop. W or Space wall-jumps or pulls you onto a grabbed ledge.

Your climbWalk through gaps in the side walls, jump through ceiling openings, or drop through floor openings. Defeat the boss in the final room to reach the next mountain section. Item and shop rooms end separate branching paths and remain placeholders.
